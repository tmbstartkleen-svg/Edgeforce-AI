# EdgeForce V194 — Launch Review + Design Freeze

V194 closes the current UX roadmap with launch-focused polish only.

## Final launch surface
- Adds a compact launch-review ribbon for live data, release certification, operator health, review pressure and mobile readiness.
- Adds a keyboard skip link into Today’s Edge.
- Standardizes focus-visible treatment across interactive controls.
- Enforces mobile touch targets on primary controls.
- Adds reduced-motion support for accessibility.
- Preserves the V192 live game center and V193 today decision flow.

## Scope freeze
- No new provider/API calls.
- No changes to provider cadence.
- No model-weight or simulation changes.
- No pricing, settlement or qualification changes.
- No topology changes.
- Cloudflare remains primary.
- Vercel remains healthy manual-only standby.

## Exit criteria
V194 is intended to be the final UX/design build in this roadmap. After exact-head, exact-main and canonical production certification pass with final closure, further builds should be driven only by a concrete defect, provider integration, or requested product feature.
