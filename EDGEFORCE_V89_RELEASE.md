# Edgeforce V89 — Baseline Succession & Replacement Readiness

V89 manages succession after a V88 champion retirement.

## Capabilities
- detects when no active champion remains
- evaluates recent calibration windows for replacement stability, quality, and evidence maturity
- classifies succession state as IDLE, BUILDING, CANDIDATE, or READY
- persists succession readiness and history
- provides a validated replacement candidate for future baseline comparisons
- exposes readiness in the System dashboard

## Guardrails
V89 does not promote a new champion by itself. It validates replacement readiness while V87/V88 retain promotion and retirement authority.
