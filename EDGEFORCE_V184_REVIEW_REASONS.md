# EdgeForce V184 — Review Reasons

V184 makes the robustness review queue explain why each row needs attention.

Review-only rows can show:
- robustness fail,
- fragile robustness,
- generic review-required state, and
- material priority-rank downgrade with the number of places lost.

The reasons use existing local robustness and rank-delta evidence only.

V184 adds zero provider/network requests and does not change qualification gates or deployment topology.
