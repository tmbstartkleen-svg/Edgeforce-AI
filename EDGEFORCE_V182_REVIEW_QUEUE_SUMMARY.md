# EdgeForce V182 — Review Queue Summary

V182 makes the V181 review queue faster to operate.

- Shows the total number of rows needing review.
- Breaks the queue down by FRAGILE, FAIL, and materially DOWNGRADED signals.
- Replaces the queue dropdown with one-click All / Review controls.
- Keeps the full board as the default.
- Uses existing local robustness and rank-delta evidence only.
- Adds zero provider/network requests.
- Does not change qualification gates or deployment topology.
