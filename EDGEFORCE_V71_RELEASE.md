# Edgeforce V71 — Unified Intelligence, Validation & Production Hardening

V71 closes the V62–V70 intelligence program by making the stack self-auditing and fail-soft.

## Unified market intelligence
Each market receives:
- unified intelligence score
- critical coverage score
- player-stack coverage
- environment coverage
- market-signal coverage
- explicit ready / not-ready state

Low intelligence coverage reduces confidence and stake sizing. It cannot silently create stronger recommendations.

## System certification
V71 checks durable health for:
- intraday injuries
- player feature frames
- player calibration
- opponent matchup learning
- role redistribution
- starting lineups / depth charts
- schedule / travel / fatigue
- venue / weather conditions
- market movement / CLV
- cross-sport optimizer
- validation
- automation

## Production hardening
- Injury automation becomes a required monitored job.
- Release identity advances to V71 and migration v83.
- Production certification consumes unified-intelligence state.
- Strict readiness fails closed on blocked intelligence, stale required automation, migration mismatch, validation failure, or incomplete release attestation.
- Optimizer and optional intelligence layers remain fail-soft: unavailable evidence cannot fabricate confidence.

V71 is the final bundle in the V62–V71 intelligence stack.
