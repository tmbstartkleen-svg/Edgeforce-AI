# Edgeforce V77 — Predictive Incident Risk & Preventive Warnings

V77 converts V76 recurrence learning into a forward-looking 24-hour operational risk forecast.

## Capabilities
- predicts the most likely next incident cause
- blends recurrence, acceleration, ACTION frequency, current health pressure, and co-failure evidence
- ranks component-level incident risks
- emits LOW / ELEVATED / HIGH / CRITICAL risk levels
- raises a preventive warning at high risk
- persists risk snapshots for audit and later calibration
- exposes preventive actions in the System dashboard

## Guardrails
Predictive risk is advisory. It cannot place wagers, promote models, reopen SLO freezes, or bypass rollback and protective-mode controls.
